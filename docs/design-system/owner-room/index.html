Room for the owner

Two new settings per node, for a node that is also someone's PC: cores and memory it keeps for its owner (keep_cores, keep_memory, nothing by default). Jobs that say their size share only what is left; a job with no size still takes one slot. Three ways to show and change them in the node's settings window, on its Jobs tab. The window itself stays as it is.

Fable's pick: A. It is the window's own pattern, the one "Space for mulebox" already uses for two values that belong together, so each value keeps its suggestion, its unsaved and pending marks and its own "Drop it", and the line under Yours says what is left for jobs. C's two bars are the clearest picture of the result and could later replace that line in A; alone, C gives the window a second way to change a setting.

One "What jobs get" line with a Change button

The result first: the node's cores as boxes and its memory as a bar, the part kept for you hatched, and one sentence. The two values stay out of sight until Change opens them in place; Save is the window's own.

2 cores and 4 GB kept. Six boxes and three quarters of the bar are for jobs.
