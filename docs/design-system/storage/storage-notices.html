Storage notices

Two things mulebox says about storage and then waits. A suggestion: a project ran no job for 14 days, clean its latest folder? And a warning: the disk is at its reserve, the rules could not free enough, here is what mulebox would remove; you decide. The example is build-1 a few days on: 44 GB free, 5 GB under its reserve of 49 GB.
