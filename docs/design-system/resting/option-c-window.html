The Health tab of a node's settings

Beside Jobs and Storage. On top the state and Check now; then each check with what it found, the failing one with its fix; last the one setting, the least free space. A mark on the tab says something fails.

This tab can go with option A or B as well: it is the only place that shows the checks that pass, and where min_free is set.
