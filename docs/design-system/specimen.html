Gauge

A bench instrument: hairlines, small corners, thin meters and tight type, with the teal mulebox already has kept for what you can act on. A live monitor earns trust by being exact, so this one fits more on a screen and stays legible by ordering it, not by decorating it.

Chart colors

Charts with more than one series (the Statistics page) take these six in this fixed order, never cycled; a seventh series folds into Other. Muted on purpose, to the level of the rest of the page: series 1 is the teal of every single bar, series 2 a dull ochre, and none of them is a status color, so a series is never read as a result. The ochre is for marks and swatches only, never for an icon or text. The single CPU line uses the accent. Two light values sit under 3:1 on a white panel, and the ochre does on a grey card, so every series also gets a label or a legend with its number, as here.

Corners

Small and stepped by size. One radius per level.

--radius-s: 4pxMenu items, counts, tooltips; meters use 3
--radius-m: 6pxButtons, icon buttons, cards, strips
--radius-l: 8pxPanels and menus

Borders and elevation

Hairlines do the separating: 1px --border around panels and between rows, 1px --border-strong around anything you can press. Cards are one tone off the panel, with a hairline.

No shadows, except --shadow-2 under a menu, the one thing that floats above the page.

Motion

--dur-fast: 100ms for hover and press, --dur-base: 180ms for a meter moving to a new value and a menu opening.

By themselves only the running icon turns and meters glide. With reduced motion the icon stands still and values change at once. Nothing fades in on load.

Icons

Lucide, outline only, stroke 1.5 at 16px (1.75 on status icons, so a state outweighs a decoration). Sizes 14, 16, 18 and 20. An icon alone always has a tooltip and an accessible name. Targets are 28 to 32px with a pointer and 36px on touch screens.

Components

Every piece below is built only from the tokens above. Switch the theme at the top of the page to see the other half.

Rules

Do

  • Separate with hairlines and one tone step. Align numbers to the right, in tabular figures.
  • Put status color on the icon only. The word beside it stays in text color.
  • Keep teal for what you can act on and for meters. One primary button per view at most.
  • Use mono for what is typed or machine-made: logs, branches, commands, positions.
  • Let a panel show less. If a row needs a fourth fact, it goes to the job's page.

Don't

  • No tinted backgrounds for states, and no colored card borders except the strips.
  • No text under 11.5px, and no facts squeezed into columns narrower than their words.
  • No filled icons, no dots standing in for states, no control drawn in --border alone.
  • No shadows on panels or cards.
  • No second accent. If something needs to shout, it is a status.

Contrast

Computed with the WCAG 2 formula from the token values, not judged by eye: 64 pairs, 64 pass. Text needs 4.5:1; icons, control outlines and meter fills need 3:1. Hairlines around panels are decoration and sit near 1.4:1 on purpose; anything you can press uses --border-strong instead. Teal and the passed green are close for some color vision, which is why a state is always an icon with its own shape, never a colored dot.
