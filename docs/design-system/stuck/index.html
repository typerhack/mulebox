A job that looks stuck

Mockups of 2026-11-15 for M24.8, to pick from before anything is built. The coordinator gives every running job one verdict from what it can see of it: working, slow, waiting, asking or stuck, with a sentence in plain words. The question here is how the dashboard shows it, so that the owner understands the situation from the card.

A

The icon, with a tooltip

The verdict's icon takes the place of the turning one; hover, focus or a tap shows the sentence.

  • Card unchanged in size.
  • Reason in a tooltip, on request.
  • Ways on as today: the page, the three dots.
 B

A line on the card

Fable suggests

The icon with two or three words and one figure, always shown; it opens in place to the sentence and the two ways on.

  • Card one line taller.
  • Reason in words on the card, the sentence one tap away.
  • Ways on on the card: its log, cancel.
 C

A mark, the evidence and the strip

A mark beside the three dots opens a panel with every reading; the strip under the header counts the jobs that will not go on.

  • Card one more icon beside the three dots.
  • Reason in a panel, with what it is based on.
  • Ways on in the panel; seen from every page.

Why B. The ask was that the owner understands the situation from the dashboard. Only B says it in words on the card with no hover or tap, which also makes it the one that works the same on a phone; it follows a pattern the card already has (the Down meter's "No bytes for 75 s"), and it keeps the turning icon, since the job is in fact still running. A is the lightest but shows only that something is off. C explains the most and costs the most; its strip could be added to B later if stuck jobs go unseen on other pages.

Every option shows the five verdicts on running cards with one opened, the stuck job's page, and a 390 px screen. The rest of the dashboard is as today. Static pages with example data: build-1 is a node, the projects and branches are examples. Colours, type, space and corners come from tokens.css; the icons are Lucide, outline.
