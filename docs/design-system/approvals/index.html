Mockups of 2026-11-15, to pick from before anything is built. From 80% of a node's space for mulebox, an agent is told to ask its user, and with a yes sends mulebox storage request --size 30GB --reason "...". The request waits on the coordinator for the owner. Two things to decide: where the owner sees and answers it, and the window where the owner says whether agents must ask at all.

The Agents window

Ask me first, or Allow, and record it: for every node, opened from the header. With what the prompt becomes under Allow, for A, B and C.

  • Opens from a new header button.
  • With Allow, a grey line tells what was changed, with Undo. Fable suggests A's strip
  • History says "applied without asking, as you allowed".

Every option shows one waiting request, several waiting, approve with another value, and the decided state in the history. Fable's report has the reasoning and the open questions.
